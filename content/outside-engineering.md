# Outside of Engineering

Outside of engineering, I have a strong interest in Kashmiri and Urdu poetry, particularly ghazals. I both read and write poetry, including my own Urdu/Roman Urdu verses. What draws me to the form is the combination of language, imagery, musicality, and the ability to express something emotionally complicated in very few words. I particularly enjoy the way a ghazal can move between melancholy, longing, existential reflection, and beauty without needing to resolve any of them. I also have an interest in the Sufi literary tradition and the relationship between poetry, introspection, and spiritual thought.

I've also been getting into painting and drawing. I don't currently treat it as a serious artistic pursuit or a discipline I'm trying to master; I mainly enjoy the act of sitting down and making something without the constant problem-solving associated with software. It's a slower, more physical kind of activity and gives me a reason to step away from a screen.

Kashmir itself is another major interest outside work. I enjoy trekking and being outdoors, particularly the combination of physical effort, mountain terrain, and the opportunity to spend time away from screens and the usual pace of everyday life. The landscape is obviously part of the appeal, but I also like the simplicity of having a small set of immediate concerns: walking, navigating the terrain, eating, resting, and getting where you're going.

I also have a passing interest in trauma psychology as a subject. I'm interested in how experiences can shape patterns of thought, behaviour, memory, and relationships, and in the broader question of how people make sense of difficult experiences.
