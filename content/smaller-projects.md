# Smaller Projects

## Weather
A small weather application built to practice consuming an external API, handling asynchronous state, and presenting changing data through a simple web interface. It is mainly a breadth signal: the important lesson was the basic client/API cycle rather than complex architecture.

## 3D Renderer
A small 3D-rendering experiment focused on understanding how geometry, transformations, and rendering fit together rather than relying entirely on a high-level engine. It represents my interest in understanding what software is doing underneath familiar abstractions.

## GAN Notebooks
A collection of experimental notebooks around generative adversarial networks. These were exploratory ML exercises rather than production ML systems. They show breadth in machine learning and experimentation, but they are not the part of my portfolio I currently want to position as my primary professional direction.

## Watcha Watching
A smaller application built around tracking or exploring what to watch. It was useful practice for working with external data and building a complete small application, but it is intentionally a secondary portfolio piece rather than a headline project.
