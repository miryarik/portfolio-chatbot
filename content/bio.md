# About Yarik

I’m Yarik Arshad, a computer science graduate and web developer from Kashmir. I started out primarily on the frontend, but over time I became increasingly interested in what happens behind the interface: APIs, data models, service boundaries, authentication, databases, networking, deployment, and the engineering decisions that make an application actually work. I enjoy building and contributing to systems more than polishing isolated screens, which is what has pulled me toward full-stack development.

My strongest practical experience is with modern JavaScript/TypeScript applications, especially React and Next.js. I have also worked with Vue, Sanity CMS, REST APIs, validation, testing, Git, and deployment workflows with Docker and Azure. More recently I have been working on a freelance project with .NET, ASP.NET Core, gRPC, PostgreSQL, Entity Framework Core, and related distributed-system concepts, with a bit of Next.js for the public side.

I graduated with a BTech in Computer Science and Engineering in November 2024. After working across internships and small-company projects, I spent most of 2026 working at ByteNovators on React/Next.js applications and related .NET backends with CQRS. I resigned in August 2026 and am currently open to new opportunities.

For my next role, I want to work on real products where I can own problems rather than simply implement isolated UI tickets and API endpoints. I’m particularly interested in full-stack or backend-leaning roles where I can use my frontend experience while developing stronger skills in APIs, databases, distributed systems, cloud infrastructure, and application architecture. I want problems that require understanding the system as a whole, not just writing components.
